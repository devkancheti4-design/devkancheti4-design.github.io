Detect Law On the RAID leaderboard

Spots AI writing.

Most detectors learn from AI text, so they go stale when new models arrive. Detect Law learns only what human writing looks like. It reads two small open models and decides with a statistical test written in integer-only C. It runs offline on a laptop.
